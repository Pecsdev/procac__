Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Answers about security, reliability, and using ProCAC with the CAC portal.

Does this app require my official CAC account password?
No. ProCAC never asks for, stores, or accesses your official CAC CRP login credentials. You log in to your CAC account as usual; the extension or bookmarklet fills fields on your active browser tab.
What happens when CAC updates their CRP portal fields?
Our service monitors field selectors on pre.cac.gov.ng and crp.cac.gov.ng. When registration forms change, field mappings are updated so your extension can target the correct inputs.
Is client data such as NIN, phone, and address securely stored?
Saved client profile records are protected with database encryption, helping keep sensitive details such as NINs and private phone numbers protected and available to your account.
Will ProCAC store or share my clients' confidential details?
Your client data is yours. Client details are used to support your filing workflow and are not sold or used for third-party marketing.
What happens if the official CAC CRP portal goes down or changes?
Portal availability and field changes are outside ProCAC’s control. We maintain field mappings as the portal evolves; please review each filing on the official portal before submission.
